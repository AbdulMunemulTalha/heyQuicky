Questions sellers ask us

Will the AI say something wrong to my customers?+

It only answers from your catalog, prices and knowledge book — never made-up prices, stock or discounts. When it isn’t sure, it asks or hands the chat to your team. And it stays off until you switch it live, so you can test it in the test chat before any real customer sees it.

Do I have to share my Facebook password?+

No. You connect through Facebook’s own official login, the same way you would connect any Meta-approved app.

Can my team still reply themselves?+

Always. Tap Take over, or type a single dot from Messenger or Business Suite, and the AI goes quiet. Type another dot to hand it back.

Which languages does it understand?+

Bangla, Banglish and English, including voice notes and mixed spelling like "vaiya L size ase?". You choose how it addresses customers — Apu, Bhaiya, Sir or Madam.

Which couriers and payments work?+

Steadfast and Pathao for booking and tracking, and bKash or Nagad transaction IDs for advances. More couriers are on the way.

What happens if my subscription ends?+

The AI pauses and your team gets a heads-up. Your inbox, saved replies and orders keep working, and the AI comes back as soon as you renew.

How long does setup take?+

About 10 minutes for most shops. If you’d rather not do it yourself, or you’re moving from another tool, we’ll set it up for you free.

Who can see my chats and data?+

Only you and the teammates you invite, with the role you give them. You can export or delete your data whenever you want.
